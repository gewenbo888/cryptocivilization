# Zero-Knowledge Civilization · 零知识文明

A bilingual (EN / 中文) systems-level platform on zero-knowledge proofs, cryptographic trust, decentralized consensus, and the trust evolution from kinship to mathematical civilization. A companion site to [Psy Protocol](https://psy.psyverse.fun) and to the parallel essay at [zk-civilization.psyverse.fun](https://zk-civilization.psyverse.fun) — this site leans more into mathematical foundations (interactive primitives) and cypherpunk culture, while the sister site is more Psy-Protocol-companion-focused.

The site is single-page, with a phosphor-green-on-black "lab notebook" aesthetic, and includes an animated SVG prover-verifier-recursion diagram, a six-rung trust evolution ladder (tribal → religious → imperial → institutional → computational → cryptographic), a five-system consensus comparison table, four privacy/transparency cards, seven ZK ideology narratives (A through G), six cypherpunk culture norms, a four-card Psy-Protocol-and-recursion section, a six-primitive Mathematical Foundations Laboratory (finite fields, elliptic curves, Merkle trees, polynomial commitments, bilinear pairings, interactive proofs — each with intuition examples), six future scenario cards, a six-dial civilization simulator that maps configurations onto known trust archetypes, and six risks-and-contradictions cards.
